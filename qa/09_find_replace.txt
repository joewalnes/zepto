================================================================================
09 — Find & Replace
================================================================================

Covers: Ctrl+F find mode, replace mode (Tab), regex toggle (⌃R), case
toggle (⌃C), capture groups ($0-$3), match count, next/prev navigation,
jump to first match.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ID:       QA-FIND-001
NAME:     Ctrl+F opens find bar
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  find

STEPS
  1. Open file with content.
  2. Press ⌃F.

VERIFY
  - Find bar appears at the bottom.
  - Label "Find:" visible, input field focused (cursor in it).
  - Status bar shows find-context pills: `.* ⌃R`, `Aa ⌃C`, `✗ Esc`, `✓ Enter`.
  - Replace field is NOT visible (find-only mode).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
