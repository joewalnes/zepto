ID:       QA-TAB-001
NAME:     Open two files shows tab bar with two tabs
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  tab

STEPS
  1. ./zepto /tmp/a.txt /tmp/b.txt

VERIFY
  - Tab bar shows two tabs, each rendered as a solid filled "pill": both
    edges capped with a full-block glyph (█) and the whole tab body —
    caps included — filled with one continuous background color. This is
    the tab bar redesign from 2026-08-30 (bugs.md "Tab bar visual
    redesign") — previously inactive tabs had no background fill at all
    (just underlined plain text) and the cap glyphs were a nearly
    invisible 1-cell diagonal wedge (◢/◣); confirm neither of those old
    glyphs appears anywhere in the tab bar.
  - First (active) tab has a clearly brighter/more saturated fill color
    than the second (inactive) tab — the two must be visually
    distinguishable as separate elements at a glance, without zooming in,
    in BOTH the dark and light themes (switch via the command palette's
    "Theme: Dark"/"Theme: Light" and re-check).
  - The inactive tab must still read as a distinct bounded rectangle
    against the surrounding tab-bar background — not just underlined
    text. (This was the core complaint the redesign fixes; if the
    inactive tab's fill blends into the bar again, that's a regression.)
  - A visible gap separates the two tabs — they must not read as one
    continuous strip.
  - Tab labels show filenames (no file-type icons in the current design).

ID:       QA-TAB-013
NAME:     Tab bar nav hints visible, labeled, and include Quit (REGRESSION)
PRIORITY: P2
FEATURE:  tab ui
RELATED:  bugs.md P2 "Non-obvious tab keys"; bugs.md P1 "Discoverability
          Contract gaps" (quit had no hint anywhere; hint was unlabeled
          bare glyphs) — QA-REG-171, QA-REG-172. Updated 2026-09-01 for the
          pill-styling conversion — bugs.md "Tab-bar buttons (close/tabs/
          quit hints) use a visually different style than the bottom
          status bar's pills" — QA-REG-229.

STEPS
  1. At least 1 tab open.
  2. Observe right side of tab bar at wide terminal (>= ~90 cols with a
     short filename).
  3. Narrow the terminal progressively.

VERIFY
  - At wide widths, the hint shows three separate rounded PILLS (rounded
    caps, filled chip background — same visual language as the bottom
    status bar's pills, e.g. "Save", "Open File"), each with a Title
    Case plain-language label next to its glyph: `⌃W Close`, `⌥←/→ Tabs`,
    `⌃Q Quit`. (Was plain, unstyled lowercase text — "close"/"tabs"/
    "quit" — with no pill shape at all; if it renders as bare text again,
    that's a regression.)
  - Quit (⌃Q) is included — it previously had no on-screen hint at all,
    anywhere.
  - As the terminal narrows past the point where labels fit, the hint
    degrades to a compact pill form (still rounded/chip-shaped, glyphs
    only: `⌃W ×`, `⌥, ← ⌥. →`, `⌃Q`) — still includes ⌃Q — rather than
    disappearing outright.
  - The three pills always appear together or not at all — narrowing the
    terminal further makes the whole group vanish in one step (blank
    fill, no truncated/overlapping glyphs, no partial 1-or-2-pill
    subset that silently drops Quit).
  - The compact pill form's floor is narrower than the old plain-text
    floor (pills need more room than dense text for the same content) —
    roughly 44-51 cols depending on tab name length and nerd-font mode
    (was a flat ~40 cols before this conversion; see tests/renderer.t for
    the deterministic, directly-measured width math). Below its floor,
    the hint blanks out honestly rather than showing garbage — quit/
    close/tab-switching remain reachable via ⌃␣ Commands regardless.
  - The FILE_TREE-context hint row (tree panel focused, wide terminal —
    see QA-TREE tests) shows the identical pill-styled hint in its own
    fallback segment — the two contexts must not drift apart.
