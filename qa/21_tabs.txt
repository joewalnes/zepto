ID:       QA-TAB-001
NAME:     Open two files shows tab bar with two tabs
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  tab

STEPS
  1. ./zepto /tmp/a.txt /tmp/b.txt

VERIFY
  - Tab bar shows two tabs.
  - First tab is active (brighter background).
  - Tab labels show filenames with icons.
