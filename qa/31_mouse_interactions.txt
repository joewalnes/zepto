================================================================================
31 — Mouse Interactions
================================================================================

Covers: click, click+drag, double/triple click, drag-to-resize tree,
scroll wheel (in editor + tree), hover, click on all pill/palette regions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ID:       QA-MS-001
NAME:     Click positions cursor in text
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  mouse click

STEPS
  1. Click at any visible position in the editor text area.

VERIFY
  - Cursor moves there.
  - Any active selection is cleared.
