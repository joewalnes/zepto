ID:       QA-MS-021
NAME:     Keyboard input renders after hover motion (REGRESSION)
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  mouse hover render
RELATED:  QA-MS-014, QA-REG-101, bugs.md 2026-08-28 P0 "Renders suppressed
          after any mouse motion"
SCRIPT:   tier1/reg_101_hover_type_render.sh

STEPS
  1. Move the mouse over the text area (no click) — send at least one
     motion event that changes no hover target.
  2. Without clicking, type a non-word character (e.g. `!`).
  3. Move the mouse again, then press Down arrow.

VERIFY
  - The typed character appears on screen immediately (no click needed).
  - The status bar cursor pill reflects the arrow-key move immediately.
  - Idle hover motion alone still does NOT trigger a render per event
    (the ?1003h motion-flood optimization is preserved).

ID:       QA-MS-023
NAME:     Scroll wheel clamps at end of file (REGRESSION)
PRIORITY: P2
FEATURE:  mouse scroll eof
RELATED:  QA-REG-221
SCRIPT:   tier1/ms_023_scroll_wheel_eof_clamp.sh

STEPS
  1. Open a file with far more lines than fit on screen.
  2. Scroll the mouse wheel down drastically -- well past the point where
     the last line first becomes visible.

VERIFY
  - The last line of the document stays pinned at the BOTTOM of the
    viewport, matching the behavior of most editors.
  - The viewport never scrolls further, leaving the last line at the TOP
    of the viewport with the rest of the pane blank underneath it.
  - Arrow-key/cursor-driven scrolling (a separate code path) already got
    this right; only the mouse-wheel path (View.pm's `scroll_down`,
    independent of cursor position) had the bug.
