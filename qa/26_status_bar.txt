26 — Status Bar
================================================================================

Covers: context-aware status bar (document/tree/find/prompt/footer), pill
types (INFO/TOGGLE/SETTING/ACTION), priority-based progressive disclosure,
mouse hover, clicks, cursor-pos pill, diff pill color, messages (no
time-based).
