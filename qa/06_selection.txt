================================================================================
06 — Selection
================================================================================

Covers: shift+arrow, shift+home/end/pgup/pgdn, word selection, select-all,
double/triple click, linear vs column semantics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ID:       QA-SEL-001
NAME:     Shift+Right extends selection one char
PRIORITY: P0
FEATURE:  selection

SETUP
  Line "abcdef", cursor at col 1.

STEPS
  1. Press Shift+Right three times.

VERIFY
  - "abc" is highlighted.
  - Cursor at col 4.
  - Status shows selected character count or similar indicator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ID:       QA-SEL-002
NAME:     Shift+Left from mid-selection shrinks
PRIORITY: P1
FEATURE:  selection

SETUP
  Selection "abc" from QA-SEL-001.

STEPS
  1. Press Shift+Left twice.

VERIFY
  - Selection shrinks to "a".
  - Cursor at col 2.

ID:       QA-SEL-005
NAME:     Shift+Alt+Right selects by word (REGRESSION)
PRIORITY: P1
FEATURE:  selection word
RELATED:  bugs.md P1 "Shift+Alt+Left/Right should select by word, not column select"

SETUP
  Line `hello world foo bar`, cursor at col 1.

STEPS
  1. Press Shift+Alt+Right once.

VERIFY
  - "hello" (or "hello ") is selected.
  - Cursor is at start of "world" or similar word boundary.
  - This does NOT enter column selection mode.
